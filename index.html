Sales Forecasting & Time Series Analysis

End-to-end retail forecasting using Python, statistics, SQL, Excel, and Power BI Python Pandas SQL Excel Power BI

Business problem

Retail businesses need reliable sales forecasts for planning inventory, staffing, purchasing, and revenue expectations. This project analyses more than one million retail transaction records and develops a time-series forecasting workflow based on historical sales patterns.

Objectives

  • Clean and prepare large-scale retail transaction data.
  • Aggregate transactions into a chronological sales series.
  • Evaluate forecasting approaches using time-based validation.
  • Measure forecast performance with appropriate error metrics.

Results

  • Processed 1,033,036 cleaned transaction lines from the UCI Online Retail II dataset.
  • Built a reproducible workflow using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, statsmodels, and SQL.
  • Compared forecasting approaches using chronological validation rather than random train-test splitting.
  • Selected a seasonal-naive forecasting approach with approximately 12% monthly MAPE for the analysed forecasting setup.
  • Documented the resulting KPIs and dashboard requirements for Power BI and Excel reporting.

Customer Segmentation

Customer behaviour analysis on 1.07 million UK e-commerce transaction lines using RFM segmentation and Power BI Python Pandas SQL Power BI

Business problem

A UK-based online giftware retailer serves a mix of consumers and wholesale buyers. Management can see total revenue, but lacks a clear view of which customers generate that revenue. The analysis segments customers by value and behaviour to support targeted marketing and retention decisions.

Key insights

  • The top 10% of customers generate 63.2% of net revenue, while the top 1% generate 31.1%.
  • Just 272 customers, representing 4.6% of the customer base, account for half of total revenue.
  • The customer revenue distribution has a Gini coefficient of 0.737, demonstrating substantial revenue concentration.
  • Champions represent 21.9% of customers but generate 69.3% of revenue, making them the highest-priority segment for retention.

Results

  • Cleaned and prepared more than one million transaction lines for analytical use.
  • Established commercial KPIs covering customers, orders, revenue, order value, and purchase frequency.
  • Applied Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scoring to classify customers.
  • Used SQL to validate analytical calculations and Power BI to communicate the resulting customer segments.
GitHub repository

05

HR Analytics

End-to-end HR analytics case study using Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel Python Pandas SQL Power BI Excel

Business problem

Acme Corporation is a fictional case-study company based on the public IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset. The analysis investigates employee attrition, identifies workforce factors associated with turnover, and translates the findings into actionable recommendations for HR management.

Key insights

  • Overall attrition is 16.12%, with 237 employees leaving out of 1,470 employees in the dataset.
  • Job satisfaction is strongly associated with attrition. Attrition reaches 22.8% for employees with a satisfaction score of 1, compared with 11.3% for employees with a score of 4.
  • Employees with fewer training opportunities show higher attrition. Attrition is 27.8% among employees with no training courses, compared with 9.2% among employees who completed 6 courses.
  • The analysis separates correlation from unsupported assumptions, helping identify practical areas for HR intervention.

Results

  • Built an end-to-end HR analytics workflow covering data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL validation, KPI analysis, and Power BI reporting.
  • Validated 65 SQL queries against Python and Pandas calculations to improve analytical consistency.
  • Produced 12 business-focused visualisations covering attrition, satisfaction, training, performance, income, and workforce patterns.
  • Developed a 3-page Power BI dashboard with interactive filters and synchronised slicers.
GitHub repository
